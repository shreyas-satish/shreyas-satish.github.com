I’m the Founder & CEO of <a href="https://www.ownpath.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ownpath</a>. We design and build products, websites, and brands for enterprises and fast-growing startups through our Studios. We've run an Academy where we’ve helped hundreds of designers, engineers, and leaders upskill and unlock interesting opportunities. We also host a Labs vertical, where we experiment, build fun things, and occasionally launch new businesses.

In my past lives, I've organized communities, designed and built ticketing software, job portals, city management platforms, and civic utilities.

Outside work, I love cooking, hosting people, going on bike rides and road trips, and escaping to the mountains or the beach whenever I can.

This site is my little corner of the internet. I write about things I’m thinking about and occasionally make games, experiments, and other things for no particularly good reason.

I love meeting new people. If you have an interesting idea, want to collaborate, or think there’s something I can help with, feel free to reach out at <a href="mailto:ss@shreyas.sh">ss@shreyas.sh</a>.
